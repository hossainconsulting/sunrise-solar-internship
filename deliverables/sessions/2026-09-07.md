### ① Twenty CF-20 tasks asserted evidence their own answers refuted

Every one of the twenty still carried its 25/08 evidence paragraph:

> *"complementary phone formats — landline on one record, mobile on the other — consistent
> with two import sources for the same customer. **That pattern holds on all 10 pairs.**"*

**It held on all 10 pairs. It predicted the right answer on 3.**

| | |
|---|---|
| Same household | **3** — Kowalski, Bennett, Young |
| Separate households | **7** — Anderson, Clark, Fitzgerald, Murphy, Patel, Singh, Tran |

Eight tasks carried a running tally written at the moment they were answered (`1 for 7`,
`2 for 8`, `3 for 9`, `3 for 10`). **Twelve carried none, and not one carried the final
figure.** So twenty closed records stood in the org asserting a hypothesis their own
answers had disproved — readable by an auditor as the basis on which they were closed.

**Fixed:** dated closing note appended to all twenty. **The original paragraph is struck,
not deleted** — the practice used everywhere in this register, because a quietly corrected
record loses the finding.

**The finding: a signal present on every member of a set cannot discriminate between
them.** Third occurrence of the substitution
[ticket-2.1-data-quality-audit.md](../ticket-2.1-data-quality-audit.md) §③/§④ records.

**And it vindicates the hold.** Merging on name similarity plus the phone pattern — which
is what that paragraph argued for — would have been **wrong 7 times in 10**, collapsing
seven pairs of records belonging to genuinely separate customers, irreversibly.

**Generated, not hand-edited.**
[build-cf-20-closing-note.py](../../scripts/build-cf-20-closing-note.py) reads each task
from the org, **derives the verdict from that task's own answer text rather than from any
document**, cross-checks it against the expected split, and refuses to emit on a mismatch,
on surviving non-ASCII, or if the note is already present. Verified: it refuses on re-run.

> **Every defect in these records since 03/09 entered through hand-editing the Comments
> box** — the mid-line splits, the mojibake, the wrong surnames, the doubled alternatives.
> Twenty records was not a volume to hand-edit a fifth time.

**One thing the generator hit and the earlier scripts had not:** anonymous Apex rejected a
single 60KB script (`Script too large`). Split into **five batches of four**, each
independently rerunnable, so a partial failure names which four to redo.

### 🔴 The list view was worse than "private" — it has no filter at all

done. **It uncovered a larger defect underneath it.** Retrieved metadata:

```xml
<listViews>
    <fullName>CF_02_Address_confirmations</fullName>
    <filterScope>Mine</filterScope>
    <label>CF-02 · Address confirmations</label>
</listViews>
```

**There is no `<filters>` element.** The list view named for six specific tasks has **no
filter criteria whatsoever** — it renders **26 items**, every Task in the org, because
Hemayet owns all 26. It was never a CF-02 list. It is "my tasks" wearing a CF-02 label.

visibility of **their own** tasks — which for all of them is **zero**. **Making it visible
to all users changes what nobody sees.**
